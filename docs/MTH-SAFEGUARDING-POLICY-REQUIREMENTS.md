# Safeguarding Policy — Requirements (NOT a published policy)

**Status:** LEGAL / POLICY REVIEW REQUIRED  
**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Product:** My Tutoring Hub (https://www.mytutoringhub.com)  
**Important:** This document is an **internal requirements draft** for counsel / policy owners. It is **not** a live public safeguarding policy, privacy notice, or terms update. Do **not** publish as-is.

---

## Purpose

Define what a future public safeguarding / child-safety policy must cover so engineering, trust & safety ops, and legal can align — without inventing legal obligations or changing the live site’s public legal pages in this engagement.

---

## Scope of the marketplace (facts)

- Discovery marketplace: tutors list Teaching Listings; students search and contact.
- **No** managed lesson booking, calendar, video classroom, escrow, or lesson custody in product scope.
- Lesson arrangements and payments are **direct** between student and tutor (0% lesson commission).
- Platform products: messaging/contact entitlements, past papers, optional tutor growth tools, identity verification review.

---

## Required policy topics (for legal drafting)

1. **Minimum age / who may use the service**  
   - Account holder age; under-18 students; parental / guardian consent thresholds.  
   - **STOP:** Engineering must not invent a public age rule until legal signs off.

2. **Parental / guardian involvement**  
   - When parents must manage accounts or approve contact.  
   - How tutors should handle under-18 learners in private arrangements.

3. **Prohibited conduct**  
   - Harassment, grooming, sexual content involving minors, scams, off-platform coercion, doxxing.  
   - Align with existing message moderation categories where helpful.

4. **Reporting**  
   - In-product Report User (structured categories).  
   - External escalation paths (law enforcement / hotlines) — jurisdiction-specific; legal to supply.

5. **Blocking & account restriction**  
   - Peer Block User; admin suspend; verification / listing removal.  
   - Appeal path for wrongly restricted users.

6. **Identity verification**  
   - Identity Verified badge is **earned** after review; Priority Verification Review is **queue only**, not a purchased badge.  
   - Document retention / deletion for ID uploads — privacy counsel.

7. **Reviews & reputation**  
   - Student reviews vs recommendations; anti-abuse eligibility.  
   - Review removal / dispute process — **STOP** for legal disputes policy.

8. **Data protection intersection**  
   - Cross-link to Privacy Policy; children’s data; retention of reports/blocks.

9. **Operator responsibilities vs user responsibilities**  
   - Platform is not a school; tutors are independent; lessons are private arrangements.  
   - Clear “what we do / don’t do” so public copy stays truthful.

10. **Incident response**  
    - Internal SLA for OPEN reports; escalation matrix; audit logging.
